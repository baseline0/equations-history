Michaelis-Menten Equation

Preview: not reviewed for publication.

\[v = \frac{S V_{max}}{K_{m} + S}\]

Units: left-hand side umol / min; right-hand side umol / min.

Assumptions

  • Quasi-steady-state assumption (d[ES]/dt ≈ 0)

History

Michaelis and Menten (1913) derived the hyperbolic rate law for an enzyme-catalysed reaction under a quasi-steady-state assumption for the enzyme-substrate complex. Briggs and Haldane (1925) reached the same law by a steady-state argument, and it is still the default model of enzyme kinetics.

First published: unknown.
