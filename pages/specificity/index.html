Catalytic Efficiency

\[k_{cat}/K_m = \frac{k_{2}}{K_{m}}\]

Units: left-hand side mM^-1 s^-1; right-hand side s^-1 / mM.

Assumptions

  • Low substrate concentration regime

History

At low substrate concentration most enzyme is free, so the rate is k_cat / K_m times the enzyme and substrate concentrations. The ratio is therefore a second-order rate constant for free enzyme reacting with substrate, which is why it is used to compare enzymes acting on the same substrate.

First published: unknown.

Citations
